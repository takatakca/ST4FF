## 1. Who does what

ST4FF by TAKATAK is a scheduling, time-clock and team-communication tool that Clover merchants use with their employees. The merchant (the employer) is responsible for its employees' information within the employment relationship; TAKATAK provides the technology and processes the information described below **on the merchant's behalf**, under its instructions. This policy describes that processing. It covers employees of merchant users and, where applicable, job applicants at those merchants.

## 2. Information we collect

ST4FF processes only the information its functions require:

- **Identity and role (synced from Clover)** — employee name, role/position, and whether an employee code exists. The code (PIN) itself is never sent to TAKATAK or stored by the App: verification happens inside Clover, on the device.
- **Contact details** — the employee's email address and phone number, provided by the merchant or the employee, used to send schedules, shift reminders and messages.
- **Schedules and availability** — planned and published shifts, stated availability, time-off requests, shift swaps and trades.
- **Punches and timesheets** — clock-in and clock-out times, breaks, the device used to clock in, late arrivals and automatic clock-outs. If the merchant enables clocking in from the employee's own device, the device used is recorded; geolocation is **not** enabled by default and, if the merchant enables it, it requires the employee's consent and is limited to the moment of the punch.
- **Rates and pre-payroll data** — hourly rate entered by the merchant and compiled hours, used for labour-cost reports and timesheet export. ST4FF does not pay wages and is not a payroll service.
- **Messages and announcements** — content of messages exchanged in the App, attachments, and read confirmations.
- **Hiring and onboarding** — for applicants: contact details and tracking steps provided by the applicant or the merchant; for employees: onboarding checklists, documents shared in the App, and certification deadlines entered by the merchant.
- **Health & safety screening** — answers to the start-of-shift questionnaire configured by the merchant. By minimisation, the App keeps the screening **result** (fit / follow-up) rather than symptom details, unless the merchant expressly configures otherwise.

The App **does not collect** the merchant's customers' payment card numbers: payments are processed directly by Clover. ST4FF does not access the merchant's inventory or customer information.

## 3. Why we collect this information

- Sync the merchant's employees and build schedules
- Operate the time clock (on the store's Clover device or in web mode) and produce timesheets
- Calculate labour cost and report it against the merchant's Clover sales, including the forecast shown while a schedule is being built
- Send published schedules, shift reminders and team messages
- Manage shift swaps, time-off requests and announcement read confirmations
- Track hiring, onboarding and the deadlines the merchant configures

We do **not** use this information for advertising, commercial profiling or resale, and we **never sell** personal information. One merchant's employee information is never mixed with another merchant's or used for another merchant.

## 4. Consent and basis for processing

The merchant, as employer, is responsible for informing its employees about the use of ST4FF and obtaining the consents required by law, in particular for email or phone reminders, for clocking in from a personal device, and for any location feature. An employee may withdraw an optional consent (e.g. text-message reminders) at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing that is necessary to perform the agreed work (schedule, time clock), which belongs to the employment relationship managed by the merchant. The health & safety questionnaire should collect only what the merchant considers necessary for workplace safety.

## 5. Disclosure and hosting

- Identity and sales information is read from Clover (Fiserv) systems through the access the merchant authorized; schedules, punches, messages and team records are hosted in TAKATAK's infrastructure.
- They may be hosted or processed in Canada or the United States depending on the infrastructure used; they remain protected by the measures described in Section 7 and by our providers' contractual obligations.
- Access to a merchant's information is limited to the merchant, its designated managers, and the employees concerned, each for what concerns them (an employee sees only their own schedules, punches, and messages addressed to them).
- We do not disclose information to any other third party, except where required by law or with the consent of the person concerned.

## 6. Retention

- **Punches and timesheets**: kept while the merchant uses the Service, and thereafter according to the period the merchant must observe for its legal obligations (payroll, labour standards); the merchant may export its timesheets before uninstalling the App.
- **Schedules, availability and messages**: kept for the duration of use of the Service; when an employee leaves, their account is deactivated, and their information is deleted or anonymized at the request of the merchant or the employee, subject to the merchant's legal obligations.
- **Health & safety screening results**: kept for a maximum of 30 days, then destroyed, unless the merchant has a different, justified configuration.
- **Applications**: kept for the period configured by the merchant (12 months by default), then destroyed.

## 7. Security

We apply safeguards proportionate to the sensitivity of employee information: encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), restricted role-based access, authentication of administrative access, and logging. No system is infallible; in the event of a confidentiality incident presenting a risk of serious harm, we will notify the merchant, the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec and the individuals concerned, in accordance with Law 25, and we will keep the incident register required by law.

## 8. Your rights (Law 25 and applicable laws)

Every employee or applicant has the right to:

- Access their information and obtain a copy
- Have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected — **including an incorrect punch or timesheet**
- Withdraw an optional consent (reminders, location)
- Request deletion of their information, subject to the merchant's legal obligations (e.g. keeping payroll records)
- Request their information in a structured technological format (portability), where the law provides for it
- File a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec

The merchant remains primarily responsible for its employees' requests; TAKATAK assists the merchant and acts on requests sent to it directly (see Section 9).
